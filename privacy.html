Privacy Policy

Application: LineBot Client01 Backup

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Purpose

LineBot Client01 Backup is an administrator-operated utility intended to store and retrieve database backup files using an authorized Google Drive account.

2. Google Account Access

The utility uses Google OAuth to request access to Google Drive. The requested permissions are used for authorized backup file upload, retrieval, listing, and management.

3. Information Processed

The utility may process Google OAuth authorization credentials, Google Drive file metadata, and database backup files selected by the authorized administrator. Backup files may contain application records and customer-related information.

4. Data Storage and Security

Google Drive files are stored within the Google account authorized by the administrator. Local configuration and temporary backup files may be stored on the administrator's computer.

Access to these files should be restricted to authorized personnel. This public website does not host database backups or OAuth credentials.

5. Data Sharing

The utility is not intended to sell Google user data or share it with advertisers. Google Drive is used as the selected storage provider. Data may be accessed by the authorized administrator and the storage provider in accordance with their applicable terms and policies.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Backup retention is determined by the administrator's backup policy. Backup files can be deleted from the authorized Google Drive account by the administrator, subject to applicable legal and contractual obligations.

7. Revoking Access

The administrator can revoke the application's Google Account access from Google Account third-party connections . Revoking access prevents future authorized API operations until authorization is granted again. It does not automatically delete previously stored backup files.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the utility's data processing or storage practices change.

9. Contact

For questions about this utility or its data practices, contact the application administrator through the support email identified in the Google OAuth application information.
